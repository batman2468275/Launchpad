Refunds & Policies

Straightforward policies, no surprises.

This page exists so you know exactly where you stand before you book a call — not to bury you in legal language.

2 rounds

Of revisions before final payment.

30 days

Website fee — paid once, not monthly.

✏️

Revisions

Two full rounds of revisions are included before final payment is collected. Use them to adjust copy, photos, layout, or anything else about your preview until it's right.

🩹

Post-launch fixes

For 30 days after your site goes live, we'll fix anything that's genuinely broken at no charge. This covers bugs, not new features, redesigns, or additional pages — those are a separate conversation.

💳

Payment

Whichever tier you choose — Base, Premium, or Ultra Premium — the website fee is a one-time payment. We only collect it after you've reviewed and approved the finished build, not before.

🌐

Domain ownership

On the Ultra Premium tier, your custom domain is registered under your own account from day one, so you retain full ownership. Launchpad covers the first year's registration cost; after that, renewal is billed to you directly at cost.

📣

Echo Studio (if bundled)

If you add Echo Studio, our separate social media service, it runs on a 3-month minimum commitment, then month-to-month after that. If you cancel, there's no refund for months already paid, but you also won't be billed for any remaining months — and there's no collections process, ever.

Questions about any of this?

Happy to walk through it on a call, no pressure either way.
