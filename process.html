Process

Here's exactly how we get you live.

No surprises, no vague timelines. This is the full path from first contact to a finished website — and what happens after.

1
Usually same day

Initial contact

You reach out — book a call or send a message. We ask a few quick questions about your business so we know what to build.

2
2–3 business days

We build a real preview

We build an actual working preview of your site — not a mockup, not a template with your name swapped in. You see the real thing before you owe us anything.

3
~30 minute call

Meeting to finalize details

We walk through the preview together, lock in copy, photos, hours, and contact details, and talk through anything you'd like changed.

4
Included, no extra charge

Two rounds of revisions

We refine layout, wording, and images based on your feedback. Two full rounds are included before anything is finalized.

5
Once you approve

Payment

Once you're happy with the build, we collect the one-time fee for your tier. Nothing is charged before you've approved the final site.

6
3–5 business days from approval

Launch

We publish your site and make sure everything works end to end — links, contact info, and (for Ultra Premium) your domain, email, and texting number.

7
30 days, free

Post-launch fixes

If anything is actually broken in the first 30 days after launch, we fix it at no charge. After that, we're still around — just ask.
